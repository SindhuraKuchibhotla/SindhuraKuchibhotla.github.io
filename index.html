SJSU · EE 295 · 2026

Fixed-point depth on a Zynq FPGA

How much precision does an on-device depth network need, and what should run on the ARM cores versus the FPGA fabric? For FastDepth on a Zybo Z7-10, I evaluated the precision tradeoffs and accelerated the first convolution layer. Built as an INT8 kernel in Vitis HLS, it runs in 4.02 ms on the board: 11.7× faster than ARM NEON, and bit-exact.

These are the times measured on the board. Hover or tap a bar to compare.

SJSU EE 277 · team of four · I lead · in progress

Hindsight Lamp

An overhead lamp with a camera that remembers where things were left. When something is put down or moved, the FPGA notices the change and the ARM core records what it was, where and when. Press the item's key and a pan-tilt spotlight lights it up, while the HDMI screen shows when it was last seen. It never records video, only a short log of changes.

My piece: I'm leading the team and own the change detector (reference frame in BRAM, event FIFO and interrupt to the ARM), the item map on the ARM, and the Vivado block design and integration.

SJSU EE 271 · team of four · in progress

Pipelined fixed-point CORDIC engine

CORDIC computes sine, cosine and rotations using only shifts and adds, no multipliers. Our team is building a parameterized, pipelined version in SystemVerilog for motor control, with a PPA and accuracy study.

My piece: the parameterized barrel shifter that does every 2−i step. Watch the vector converge.

Monocular depth estimation

Seeing depth from a single camera, the way a car or robot has to when there's no second lens or LiDAR to lean on. At IISc, our group of four compared four U-Net encoders on NYU Depth V2 and chose a DenseNet-201 U-Net with a custom SSIM, edge and cosine loss.

Object detection with voice feedback
