SENSE · perception
IISc Bangalore · thesis

Monocular depth estimation

Seeing depth from a single camera, the way a car or robot has to when there's no second lens or LiDAR to lean on. My U-Net with a DenseNet-201 encoder reached 85.81% test accuracy, above the 85% published benchmark, and I'm now quantizing it for an FPGA.

Drag the line across the street to switch between what the camera sees and the depth a model would estimate.

SJSU EE 271 · team of four · in progress

Pipelined fixed-point CORDIC engine

CORDIC computes sine, cosine and rotations using only shifts and adds, no multipliers. Our team is building a parameterized, pipelined version in SystemVerilog for motor control, with a PPA and accuracy study.

My piece: the parameterized barrel shifter that does every 2−i step. Watch the vector converge.

About

Engineer, mentor, forever curious.

I'm Sindhura, an electrical engineer who took the long way round. I spent years making models smart, and now I'm learning how to make them small, fast and efficient enough to live inside real devices.

I like work you can hold: a board on the bench, a camera that actually sees, an LED that lights up because a model decided it should. Long term, I want to design the hardware that AI runs on.
